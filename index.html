Hands-on problem solving, documented.

Tesla Model S P85 — battery & drivetrain rebuild

Complete disassembly, refurbishment, and reassembly of the 85 kWh high-voltage pack and drivetrain on a 2014 Model S with 260,000 miles. Sixteen modules, 400V+, a custom corrosion treatment — and the car is back on the road.

Plane Pointer — ADS-B aircraft tracker

A 2-axis servo system that physically points at live aircraft overhead using ADS-B data, with a companion display showing flight ID, position, altitude, speed, and heading. A pilot's desk toy that does real math.

  • ADS-B
